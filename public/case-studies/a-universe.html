The Origin

Windsor, Ontario and Detroit, Michigan share one river and one sky. Jazmine Mackenzie has always lived on both sides — not split between them, but whole on each bank simultaneously. One river, both banks. That's not a border, that's a bridge. Everything she makes carries that frequency: the refusal to experience belonging as a choice.

The Practice

Eighteen years with a comb. A published poet. A gratitude framework creator. A web designer and brand strategist. A builder of digital assets for the AI era. A vocalist and songwriter. A teacher. The question was never which one to choose. What happens when someone refuses to collapse into one thing? The answer — everything she touches becomes more whole.

Bio — Long Form
Jazmine Mackenzie is a creative force from the Windsor–Detroit corridor — a border baby who has always lived on both sides of one river, and never stopped expanding in every direction at once. Eighteen years as a hair artist have earned her chairs at editorial shoots, runway productions, and wedding ceremonies. She is a published poet and the creator of a gratitude framework that lives at the intersection of inner work and practical design. A web designer and brand strategist behind Infinite Spaces. A builder of digital assets for the AI era. A vocalist and songwriter whose music is the overflow of a creative mind that refuses to be contained on the page alone. She is not what you'd call a multihyphenate. That word implies a list — and a list has an end. Jazmine Mackenzie is a universe: self-contained, still expanding, containing everything.

08 — Design Principles

Rules for Building the Dream

01
The Dream Is the Ground
The atmosphere is always soft — lavender sky, pearl mist, rose chrome. The void lives underneath as depth and shadow layer. You enter the dream. You don't observe it from outside.
02
Warm Chrome Only
Every metallic surface carries rose, violet, or gold within it. This is a warm universe — it has a body temperature. Industrial cold chrome is the opposite of the vision.
03
Everything Breathes
Nothing on this site is static. Scroll reveals, hover states, portal transitions — all move with intention. The site should feel like a living organism. Slow. Deliberate. Alive.
04
Words Lead, Craft Follows
Copy is a second creative act, not a caption. Every headline earns its space. Write like a poet who also codes — precision over performance. The border city cadence underneath always.
05
The Ether Is Devoted
The tech portal is not a portfolio. It is a digital plane of existence. Keyboards as altars. Monitors as portals. Rose quartz on circuitry. Code as devotion, never as mere mechanics.
06
The River Runs Through It
Windsor and Detroit are always present — grit under the beauty, directness inside the poetry, Motown underneath the chrome. She is from somewhere real. The site should feel it.
